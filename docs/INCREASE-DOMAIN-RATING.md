## What is Domain Rating?

Domain Rating (DR) is Ahrefs' relative measure of a domain's backlink profile on a 100-point scale. It considers the strength of referring domains and how many other domains they link to. A directory's high score alone does not establish link quality or predict your own DR change. [Source: Ahrefs' DR definition](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/1409408-what-is-domain-rating-dr).

## A directory submission workflow

1. **Build a relevant shortlist.** Select destinations that accept your type of product and serve its audience. The full archive also includes communities and media sites, so read the category notes.
2. **Open the exact destination.** Check its current submission path, eligibility, cost and moderation rules. Keep different paths on the same domain identifiable in your tracker.
3. **Submit accurate product details.** Use your real product name, URL, category and an original description. Follow each site's rules and avoid duplicate submissions or unrelated community promotion.
4. **Check the published result.** Record the live listing URL, whether it links to your website, and the link's current attributes. A submitted form is not a published backlink.
5. **Measure outcomes over time.** Track approvals, referral visits, new referring domains and DR observations separately. Use the [CSV export](../data/directories.csv) as the starting point for a submission tracker.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can directory submissions increase Domain Rating?

A published followed backlink can contribute to the profile Ahrefs measures. Acceptance, link treatment and the resulting DR change vary. Select relevant destinations and verify the result instead of treating every row as a promised increase. [Ahrefs' explanation](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/1409408-what-is-domain-rating-dr).

### Do nofollow backlinks increase DR?

Ahrefs says nofollow backlinks do not pass rating in its DR calculation. A nofollow placement may still be worth evaluating for its audience and referral traffic. [Source: Ahrefs on DR changes](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/2061537-why-has-there-been-a-drop-in-my-domain-rating-dr-recently-did-your-crawl-algorithm-change).

### Where can I find free backlinks for a SaaS startup?

Begin with the [free dofollow shortlist](../views/free-dofollow-startup-directories.md), then narrow it by category and current eligibility. This catalog records 836 entries labeled Free across all link types; it does not promise 836 free backlinks.

### Can I boost DR quickly or guarantee a target score?

This catalog cannot promise a timeline or target score. A directory's DR is a research filter, not the number of points it will add to your site. Ahrefs also describes DR as relative to other websites. [Source: Ahrefs' DR definition](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/1409408-what-is-domain-rating-dr).
